# Results index

Benchmark ids follow the paper: synthetic `s1_*` ... `s6_*` are S1-S6, the curvature sweep is `curvature_warp_k*`, and engineering E1 = e1/bwb, E2 = e2/urban_wind, E3 = e3/acopf_ieee57, E4 = e4/chip_layout. Seeds are 0-9 unless stated. Synthetic metrics come from `pal.eval.table1_metrics`, engineering aggregates from `scripts/aggregate_engineering.py` and `scripts/render_engineering.py`. GPU runs use the tiers in `pal/sweep/gpu_presets.yaml`: `small_gpu` (up to 11 GB), `medium_gpu` (12-24 GB) and `large_gpu` (40 GB and up).
